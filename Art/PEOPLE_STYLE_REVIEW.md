# Toon people alternatives

[Open the comparison](Previews/PeopleStudy/PeopleComparison.png). Workers are above, customers below.

| Direction | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| Rounded crew | Rounded face, small oval eyes, full body, soft clothing shapes |
| Chunky comic | Taller proportions, rounded square head, larger brows and white eyes |
| Pocket people | Large head, short limbs, compact body; bob haircut on the customer |
| Floating crew | Floating hands and apron-shaped torso, with a smaller face than the earlier model |

All eight are in [IceCreamTruckWorkshop.blend](../Ice%20Cream%20Truck%20Prototype/Assets/IceCreamTruckWorkshop.blend), collection `O - Toon people alternatives`. The scene `People - compare silhouettes` arranges them side by side. The previous characters remain in the workshop.

These are geometry alternatives in the selected cel cartoon material style. The full-body models have simple fifteen-bone rigs; the floating models have five-bone rigs. Parts use rigid bone weights for posing. Animation clips, deformation polish, Unity export, and gameplay integration are still pending.

Numeric model labels were cleared from the toon kit. Ingredient names remain. The plan now specifies green/red supply bars without counts, fractions, or percentages.

The live workshop was backed up to `Source/BeforePeopleStudy_Append.blend` before changes. `Previews/PeopleStudy/append-audit.json` lists the cleared labels. `saved-master-audit.json` records preservation and rig checks against the saved workshop.
